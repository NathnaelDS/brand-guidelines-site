# Kestrel Tea

Kestrel Tea is a small-batch tea blender in Porto: whole-leaf teas from named estates, blended and packed by hand, sold in tins designed to be kept.

## Strategy

### Overview

**What it is.** A tea company "sourcing whole-leaf teas from a handful of estates we have visited, and blending them in small batches in Porto" *(PDF p.16, sample letter)*. It is registered as Kestrel Tea Co., Lda, and established in 2024 *(artwork: gift box base, badges)*.

**What it really does.** It makes good tea "ordinary in the best sense: something you can buy, understand and enjoy every day, without ceremony or snobbery" *(PDF p.3)*.

**The range today** *(artwork)*:
- Three teas, each named in English and Portuguese:
  - Smoked Black (Preto Fumado)
  - White Peony (Peónia Branca)
  - Golden Oolong (Oolong Dourado)
- Packed in 50 g and 100 g tins.
- Sold singly and in a three-tin gift box.
- Also made as private-label tins carrying a café partner's mark.

**The problem it solves** *(proposal)*. Good loose-leaf tea is either hidden behind specialist jargon or sold as anonymous dust in bags. Kestrel gives it a name, an origin and plain instructions.

**Ambition** *(proposal)*. To be the tea shelf people in Porto trust first, and the tin visitors take home.

### Audience

*Inferred from the packaging and collateral. Confirm with the brand owner.*

- **Primary: independent cafés and restaurants.** The gift box lists "Cafés · Restaurants · Hotels · Offices" *(artwork: Gift Box, Side A)*, and the private-label tins exist for this buyer *(artwork)*.
- **Secondary: considered home drinkers**, who buy one good tea at a time *(PDF p.30 sample copy: "the tin you refill, not replace")*.
- **Secondary: visitors to the city**, buying a gift *(artwork: gift box)*.
- **Stakeholders, not buyers: the estates.** The sample letter names "long relationships with growers" as a principle *(PDF p.16)*.
- **Not for** *(proposal)*: buyers choosing on price alone, and anyone who wants tea as theatre.

### Positioning

- **Category** *(proposal)*: everyday specialty tea, blended locally.
- **Line used on the guidelines' LinkedIn mockup:** "Whole-Leaf Tea, Blended in Porto" *(PDF p.49)*.
- **What it is not** *(proposal)*:
  - not a luxury tea house
  - not a wellness brand
  - not a souvenir brand
- **Structural differentials:**
  - Named estates on every tin *(artwork)*.
  - Brewing instructions in plain numbers (grams, degrees, minutes) on every tin *(artwork)*.
  - Private-label production for partners *(artwork)*.
  - A visual identity that "avoids the steam-and-teacup shorthand of every tea shelf" *(PDF p.33)*.

### Personality

- **Archetype** *(proposal)*: The Host.
- **Attributes, from the tone page:** unhurried, precise, warm, plain-spoken *(PDF p.4)*.
- **The designer's tone sliders** *(PDF p.4)*:

  - balanced between friendly and professional

- **What it is not:** precious, mystical, preachy *(PDF p.4)*.

### References & Anti-References

#### References

- **Azulejo tiles.** The logomark's counter "echoes the lattice of a tiled facade" *(PDF p.6)*.
  - Borrow the square rhythm and the blue-and-warm palette.
  - Do not borrow literal tile illustration.
- **The river warehouses.** The palette was sampled from their painted doors *(PDF p.19–20)*.
- **Rice paper.** The brand texture "comes from the paper the founders wrap samples in" *(PDF p.32)*.

#### Anti-References

- **Traditional tea branding.** Reject steam curls, teacup silhouettes and leaf sprigs as the main idea *(PDF p.33)*.
- **Wellness language.** Reject detox, cleanse and "superfood" framing *(PDF p.4)*.

### Promise

**Core promise:**

**Base message** *(PDF p.3)*: Kestrel Tea makes good tea ordinary, in the best sense.

### Guardrails

From *(PDF p.4)* unless marked:

4. No wellness claims, no mysticism, no ceremony.
5. *(proposal)* Use exclamation marks rarely.
6. *(proposal)* Write the English tea name with its Portuguese name wherever space allows.

## Open Items

Unresolved items found while auditing the source files. Each one needs a decision from the brand owner or Northfold Studio. Agents must not resolve these silently. *(Fictional, illustrating the kinds of findings an audit produces.)*

**Color**
1. **Ember Red value.** The PDF swatch is labeled `#B23A3E`. The swatch itself and all logo, tin and card artwork use `#B23A2E`. One file (the Secondary lockup SVG) still uses `#B23A3E`.
2. **Marigold value.** The PDF label repeats Ember Red's HEX, RGB and CMYK. The swatch is actually `#EFC64A`. CMYK still needs to be supplied.
3. **Colors in artwork that aren't in the palette:** `#F9F6EE` (pattern assets), `#D58F2A` (Golden Oolong tin). Decide whether to adopt them as official tints or correct the artwork.

**Packaging**
4. **The 100 g Golden Oolong tin is mislabeled** "WHITE PEONY / PEÓNIA BRANCA". Fix before printing.
5. **"OOLOONG"** is misspelled on the gift box front.
6. **"Kestral Tea"** on Gift Box Side A should read "Kestrel Tea".

**Logo**
7. **Clear space and minimum sizes** are listed in the Logo Design contents, but the pages are missing.
8. **® vs ™.** Some mockups show ®. The guidelines use ™. Confirm the trademark status.

**Type**
9. **Typography page text** refers to "Halden", a leftover from another brand's template *(PDF p.30)*.
10. **Quill Hand** is missing from the fonts folder and its license is unverified.

**Guidelines document**
11. **Index page numbers are shifted** by one section *(PDF p.2)*.

**Voice and positioning**
12. **Tagline.** No tagline is formally designated. "Tea, Taken Slowly." was chosen because the artwork repeats it. Confirm it, and fix its casing.
13. **Proposals to confirm or replace:** Audience, Positioning, Archetype, Ambition, Litmus test, We Never Say examples.
14. **Accessibility.** The source sets no accessibility commitment. The DESIGN.md linter flagged two of the designer's own pairings: Paper White on Saffron (Golden Oolong tin) is 2.42:1, and Sage on Pale Sage (badge) is 3.76:1. Decide on a target before these pairings are used for small text.

**Folder housekeeping**
15. `3 - Brand Collaterals/4 - Packaging (Master)` is a byte-identical duplicate of `4 - Packaging (Master)`.
